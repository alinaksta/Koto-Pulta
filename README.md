# Koto Pulta 

**Koto Pulta** is a comedic delivery game developed as a Innopolis University Capstone Project. Step into the shoes of a chaotic restaurant worker who browses an old-fashioned website for food, hands dishes to a jelly cat officiant, and then **throws the cat** at customers to serve them. It's absurd, fast-paced, and all about controlled chaos.

**Track:** Startup  
**Platform:** PC (Windows) – Keyboard + Mouse  
**Genre:** Casual Simulator

## Overview

In **Koto Pulta**, you manage a cafe with a **jelly cat** waiters and customers. Your job is to:

1. Walk around the restaurant.
2. Browse a retro-style website to find food.
3. Hand the food to the jelly cat officiant.
4. Pick up the cat and **throw** it at waiting customers.
5. Hit the right customer with the correct dish to earn coins.

---

## Key Features

| Features | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Movement** | WASD walking with collision on a single restaurant map. |
| **Interaction** | Interact with computer, cat, and customers using mouse buttons (LMB/RMB). |
| **Computer & Food** | Open a retro website, browse food photos, click to grab food in hand. |
| **Jelly Cat Officiant** | Give food to cat, pick it up, and throw it in a parabolic arc. |
| **Delivery** | Hit the right customer with the cat holding correct food. |
| **Audio & UI** | SFX , HUD. |
